### 2. Bias analysis

A few questions can accidentally influence the participant:

- **“What makes playing through Game Pass difficult?”** assumes there _is_ a difficulty.
- **“What would make Game Pass better?”** can push users toward product improvements rather than their actual needs.
- **“Why did you subscribe?”** may encourage a rationalised answer rather than the real decision process.
- **“How do you decide whether it is worth it?”** assumes they actively evaluate value.

### 3. Refined, neutral questions

1. **Tell me about the last time you decided what game to play. What happened?**
2. **How do you usually discover games you might want to play?**
3. **Tell me about your experience with Game Pass.**
4. **Think back to when you first subscribed. What was happening at that time?**
5. **Walk me through the last time you used Game Pass to find something to play.**
6. **Tell me about a time when Game Pass didn't have something you wanted. What did you do next?**
7. **How do you think about the money you spend on Game Pass?**
8. **Have you ever considered changing or ending your Game Pass subscription? Tell me about that experience.**
9. **Tell me about anything that has made using Game Pass different from what you expected.**
10. **If you could change anything about your experience with Game Pass, what would you change, and why?**
