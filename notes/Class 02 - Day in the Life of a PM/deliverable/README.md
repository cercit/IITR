## Session 02 — Day in the Life of a PM

**What does a Product Manager at Cars24 actually do all day?**

I built this as part of my PM coursework — pick a company, map out the real PM role, and publish it. Cars24 made sense because the used-car market sits at a messy intersection of trust, logistics, pricing, and finance. That's where product work gets interesting.

A few things that stood out while researching this:

**The metrics aren't what you'd guess.** GMV and conversion rate, sure. But the ones that separate Cars24 from a classifieds board are Listing Quality Score, Time to Sell, and Financing Penetration Rate. Those tell you whether the platform is actually solving the trust problem or just moving inventory.

**The PM here isn't just building app screens.** A Cars24 PM coordinates inspection, reconditioning, RC transfer, logistics, and financing — across online and offline touchpoints. One bad handoff and the customer's trust is gone. The product is the process, not the interface.

**The hardest challenge is building trust in a category where people default to offline.** Buying a used car is a high-consideration, low-frequency decision. Nobody impulse-buys a second-hand Hyundai. Every product decision has to reduce anxiety, not just reduce friction.
